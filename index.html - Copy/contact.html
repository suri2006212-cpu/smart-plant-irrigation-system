Quick Help

Common questions about PlantFlow AI

🌱 Do I need sensors?

No. PlantFlow AI is designed as a web-based system and does not require physical sensors.

💧 How is water calculated?

The system considers plant type, soil, sunlight, condition and pot size.

📊 Can I track water usage?

Yes. The Analytics page displays water usage, savings and plant performance.

🌤️ Can weather data be added?

Yes. A weather API can be integrated later to improve irrigation recommendations.
