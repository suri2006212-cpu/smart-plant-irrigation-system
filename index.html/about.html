ABOUT PLANTFLOW AI

Smarter Watering.
Healthier Plants.

PlantFlow AI is a web-based smart plant management platform designed to help users plan irrigation, monitor plant health, and reduce unnecessary water usage.

What is PlantFlow AI?

PlantFlow AI is a modern web application that provides intelligent plant watering recommendations based on plant type, soil condition, sunlight, plant condition, and pot size.

Instead of following the same watering routine for every plant, the system creates a personalized irrigation plan for different plants.

The main goal is simple: use the right amount of water at the right time.

🌿

Our Mission

To make plant care easier, smarter, and more water-efficient through a simple web-based intelligent management system.

How PlantFlow AI Works

A simple four-step smart plant management process

01

03
